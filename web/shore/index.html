What’s still public

    Counterparty risk

    The hop runs through NEAR Intents 1Click, a solver network with an operator. While your ZEC is in flight it can be delayed, refunded or frozen by that service. SHORE never holds your funds.

    Why now

    • Raydium’s CMO: 38,000 first-time ZEC holders on Solana in six weeks; almost none bridged.
    • ZecHub guide: today’s path is 2 sites, 2+ approvals, about 8 minutes.
    • Zooko pointed those holders at that guide.
    • Bounties pay for this onboarding: ZecHub, zkSNARKs “first shielded transaction”.

    Business model

    Holders will pay a small, transparent SHORE fee on each shield: it is built in through the 1Click appFees field and shown as its own line in the quote, and it is switched off today, so every shield so far paid only 1Click’s fees. Wallets, communities and bounty programs are the planned buyers of invite links that bring their members through SHORE in one approval; none has paid yet. SHORE never holds funds, so the fee is the whole business.
