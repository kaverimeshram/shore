Pilot: phantom-home, zashi-home, stripe-checkout were captured first and every PNG opened before the rest.

## Relay bridge (product screen, domain)
What it is: the live swap/bridge screen at relay.link/bridge, one form, sell over buy. Move worth stealing: the whole task is one 226px-wide-per-side form centred on a flat tinted field (#F7F8FF), sell and buy are two stacked blocks separated only by a 1px rule and a small arrow chip; amount at 28px, fiat at 12px under it, the single primary button full width at 14px/500. Numbers: amount first, token chip right. Empty state is "0" in muted ink. Do NOT take: the italic all-caps button label, the violet accent, the Intercom bubble.
PNG: design-research/refs/relay-bridge-1440.png, design-research/refs/relay-bridge-390.png

## Mayan swap (product screen, domain)
What it is: swap.mayan.finance, the Solana-native cross-chain swap. Move worth stealing: the From/To pair as two label-over-field rows with the asset block (ETH / Ethereum, 14px over 11px) left and the amount field right, 1px #3A3A40 borders, radius 0 on the frame; button text Space Grotesk 14px with 0.4px tracking. Numbers left-aligned in a mono at 16px. Do NOT take: the pastel gradient pill CTA, Roboto body (16px, 0.15px tracking), the dead grey 1440 field around a 255px form, boxes inside a box.
PNG: design-research/refs/mayan-1440.png, design-research/refs/mayan-390.png
