# SHORE design: "Ferry log"

Art direction: a ferry log on tide-chart paper. The left page states the argument and logs the crossing leg by leg; the right page is the register of five numbered steps, every one legible before it unlocks.

- Type: Newsreader (display, read-back amount, balance, clock: money with a history, as on z.cash) and Manrope (everything else: a warm, legible grotesk at 15 to 17px). No mono.
- Scale: 60 / 40 / 24 / 17 / 15 / 13 px. Display tracking -0.022em, leading 1.04. Small caps labels +0.04em. Tabular figures throughout.
- Colour 70/20/10: tide paper #EEF1EC; ink #0F1B18 with #3B4843 secondary (8.6:1 on paper) and rules #C8D0CA; harbor teal #0B6E63 has one job: the next action and "shielded".
- Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96. Radius 6px. Hairline rules separate sections; no card ever sits inside another.
- Motion: one orchestrated load (log rises in, 60ms stagger); reduced motion disables it.
- Refuse: cards in cards, dark default template, mono hacker figures, status pills, gold-on-navy, glow, big-metric tiles.
Sources: design-research/REFS.md (Relay, Mayan, Wise rows, Flighty legs, z.cash serif on paper, Phantom restraint).
