## Phantom (ref-phantom-home-1440.png, ref-phantom-home-390.png, ref-phantom-home-tokens.json)
DNA: Marquee hero on a tinted field; one custom grotesk (Phantom 400) at every size, h1 80px / -2px / 88px leading, h2 24px / -0.6px; hierarchy by size and tracking, never weight. Colour anchor lavender #F4F2FF at about 70%, ink #3C315B, warm white #FFFDF8 on the dark hero panel. Rhythm: big calm blocks, one CTA per screen. Motion: lottie-style hero art. Take: restraint, single family discipline, negative tracking on display. Leave: lavender identity, 32px pills, hero-as-floating-card.

## Flighty (ref-flighty-1440.png, ref-flighty-390.png, ref-flighty-tokens.json)
DNA: a journey told as legs with true times; h1 65px/78px -1px bold grotesk, black on white; long honest paragraph at 14px/16.8px 500. Promise "Get the truth when you travel" maps onto SHORE's read-back. Take: the crossing as a vertical log of legs with the elapsed time big and the caption plain. Leave: scroll-jacked marketing, blank lazy tiles.
