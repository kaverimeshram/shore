# SHORE pitch script (2:00 spoken)

Zcash track, Colosseum Solana World's Fair. Speaker: kamal. Written to stand on its own, with no video.
Only the quoted lines are spoken.

## 0:00 to 0:15, hook

> 38,000 people on Solana got paid in ZEC from rewards and had never held ZEC before. That's Raydium's
> CMO, on the Zcash forum. About half of that ZEC is still sitting in their wallets. Almost no one bridged it.

## 0:15 to 0:28, the pain

> Here's why. The ZecHub guide to shield it takes two sites, two or more approvals and about eight
> minutes. The guide's own author took 507 seconds.

## 0:28 to 1:02, the aha

> I'm kamal, and I built SHORE. Connect Phantom, paste a fresh shielded address from your Zcash wallet,
> see a quote with every fee, and approve once. That's the only signature. On mainnet, 84 seconds later,
> 115,000 zat landed in Zcash's Ironwood pool. Then SHORE reads the note back with a view-only key, so
> you see what actually landed: 7,000 zat more than 1Click's status said. Cost: about 39 cents, mostly a
> fixed withdraw fee that shrinks as the amount grows.

## 1:02 to 1:18, who it's for and the market

> It's for people paid in ZEC on Solana who never held ZEC before: about 84,000 wallets and about 8,700
> ZEC from one reward token alone. Launchpads like Sapling, SnarksPad and Billet are where you speculate.
> SHORE is the way in.

## 1:18 to 1:36, business model

> Holders pay a small, transparent SHORE fee on each shield, set through 1Click's app fee field and shown
> as its own line in the quote. Today it's zero. Wallets, communities and bounty programs like ZecHub pay
> for invite links that bring their members through in one approval. SHORE never holds funds.

## 1:36 to 2:00, honest risk, why Zcash, close

> The honest part: the hop in is public, the route links your Phantom address to that one shielded
> address, NEAR Intents once held about $589,000 for 50 days, and you need a Zcash wallet. Why Zcash?
> Because after that hop, every spend from the note stays private. Try it at shore-zec.pages.dev/shore.
