# AIRLOCK reference study

Ten real captures (refshot, 1440 wide unless noted). Tokens are in the matching .tokens.json files. Domain refs: ledgers, redaction and records tools, chain explorers. Outside refs found by the problem's shape: "a document that shows what a stranger can read" and "a value with its source beside it".

## Stripe API reference, Charge object
Screen: a field-by-field object reference with a live JSON panel beside it. Move worth stealing: each attribute is a row with the key in monospace 16px bold, its type as a small grey tag at 13px beside it, then a one-line plain description, separated by 1px hairlines at about 24px vertical rhythm. Body 14px on 22px leading. Numbers and ids are shown as literal values in a mono panel, not summarised. Primary action is absent; the page is the proof. Do NOT take: the dark navy chrome, the 8px-radius pills, the purple links, or the system-ui stack (no loaded fonts). AIRLOCK keeps the key-then-type-then-value row but sets it on paper with dotted leaders instead of hairlines. PNG: refs/stripe-docs-1440.png, refs/stripe-docs-390.png.

## Linear changelog
Screen: a dated release log. Move worth stealing: a left date column with a single 6px dot on a 1px vertical rule, entries in a 640px reading column, h1 48px at -1.056px tracking on 48px leading (tight display), h3 24px at -0.288px. Date and body are separated by whitespace only, no card. Numbers: none headline. Empty state: n/a. Do NOT take: Inter Variable, the near-black ground, 9999px pill nav, the dashed-box diagram illustration. AIRLOCK takes the dated timeline gutter for part status and the negative tracking on display type only. PNG: refs/linear-changelog-1440.png, refs/linear-changelog-390.png.

## Are.na landing
Screen: a text-first intro with a lettered list (a. b. c.) and two buttons. Move worth stealing: the page is one 636px column of 19.2px type on 27.84px leading, lettered list markers hang in the left margin, buttons are plain 14.4px bold with a 3px radius and sit directly under the copy so the call to action is on the first screen without a hero image. Do NOT take: pure black ground, the screenshot carousel, the glow behind the product frame. AIRLOCK takes the hanging markers for the three observer blocks and the single narrow column. PNG: refs/arena-1440.png.

## hledger plain text accounting
Screen: a ledger tool home with a screenshot strip and prose. Move worth stealing: the principle, not the look. The product is data that stays human-readable; the page sells a quote about "eyeball auditing" at 16px italic with a 1px rule at 90px margin. Body 16px on 23.2px leading in Open Sans. Do NOT take: the gold coin illustration, the dark theme, the 24px weight-200 headings that vanish. AIRLOCK takes the idea that a ledger line is the unit of trust and renders each field as one line. PNG: refs/hledger-1440.png.

## MuckRock public records
Screen: a records-request hub with live counts. Move worth stealing: four counts (filed requests, agencies, fulfilled, pages released) each as a number over a 14px label with no card, set at 20px over a Source Sans Pro 16px body on 23px leading, ground #F7F8FA. The text says what the tool does in the first sentence of 16px copy. Do NOT take: the pink wash photo behind the stats, blue button fill, the heart donation link. AIRLOCK takes figures-over-labels with the source call named under each. PNG: refs/muckrock-1440.png.

## mempool.space transaction page
Screen: a chain observer's view of a transaction. Move worth stealing: exactly what the chain observer sees. The txid is a long cyan mono string at 16px beside a 28px weight-500 heading, with a copy glyph; the empty state ("Transaction not found. Waiting for it to appear in the mempool...") is a centered 28px line, not a blank panel. Do NOT take: the purple 4px-radius buttons, the glowing 3D block cubes, the icon bar. AIRLOCK takes the plain not-found line as a model for its 404 state and the full-width mono id line. PNG: refs/mempool-1440.png.

## Our World in Data, grapher
Screen: a chart page with provenance. Move worth stealing: every figure carries its source directly beneath at 12px ("Data source: ...") and the title is a serif h1 at 25px weight 600 in Playfair Display over 15px Lato. The side table lists values right-aligned with a faint bar behind each, so a value and its magnitude read together. Do NOT take: the navy header, the blue-and-red palette, the rounded tab group. AIRLOCK takes source-under-figure and the faint fill behind a value, which becomes the hazard highlighter. PNG: refs/owid-1440.png.

## Edward Tufte, Graphics Press
Screen: a book page with price table. Move worth stealing: a warm ground (#FFFFF8 family cream), a serif display feel, a single oxblood accent rgb(177,40,43) used for headings and the buy button at a 2px radius with 12px bold caps. One accent, one job. Body Lato 20px on 34px leading is generous. Do NOT take: the uppercase heavy headings at 34px, the pill search box at 22.5px radius, the cart icon. AIRLOCK takes the one-accent discipline and the 2px control radius. PNG: refs/tufte-1440.png.

## Flighty
Screen: a consumer app landing built around change alerts ("Gate changed", "Delayed 45m"). Move worth stealing: h1 65px at -1px tracking on 78px leading, two-line max, subtext centered at about 20 words, and the alert strings are real sentences with a value inside, not labels. Do NOT take: system-ui as the face, the phone mockup, the faded-notification stack, the emoji in copy. AIRLOCK takes the sentence-with-a-value voice for observer blocks and the 2-line headline cap. PNG: refs/flighty-1440.png.

## Gwern.net about page
Screen: a long document with a table of contents, sidenote metadata and a boxed aside. Move worth stealing: a metadata line under the title ("certainty", "importance", dates) set in small italic, a dotted double-rule box for asides, IBM Plex Mono 16px on 25.26px leading for data, h1 50px at -1px tracking in Source Serif 4. Do NOT take: the dark mode default, the right-edge widget toolbar, the small-caps title. AIRLOCK takes the metadata strip for the receipt header and Plex Mono for data. PNG: refs/gwern-1440.png.
